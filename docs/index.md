Geometric AI goes beyond today's fixed-pipeline neural networks. Where current systems run the same architecture for every input, Geometric AI builds adaptive, modular systems where information flow is visible, measurable, and controllable at every step.

---

## What We Build

**Artificial Brain Studio** is our flagship platform for designing, training, and inspecting next-generation neural architectures. Instead of one fixed sequential network, builders create adaptive systems that grow internally and externally. Developers control where data enters, where outputs are produced, how modules communicate, where losses are applied, and how information flows, all within one visual and programmable environment.

During inference, the full internal data flow is observable. This makes AI decisions transparent, explainable, and controllable in a way that post-hoc methods cannot match.

---

## Scientific Foundation

Our technology is grounded in the **Pursuit of Subspaces (PoS)** hypothesis, an axiomatic mathematical framework that models neural reasoning as structured transformations between compact representation spaces. PoS provides a unified foundation for:

- **Explainability by design:** transparency is structural, not approximate
- **Zero-shot and few-shot reasoning:** generalisation from geometric properties alone
- **Adaptive neural computation:** dynamic routing based on input geometry
- **Geometric counterfactual explanations:** translating anomalous inputs toward normal representations
- **Privacy-preserving transformation:** removing sensitive attributes while preserving task structure
- **Controllable human-AI alignment:** steering behaviour through explicit geometric constraints

## Why Geometric AI?

Geometric AI is not just another AI model. It is a platform for building controllable, artificial brain-like systems where the internal flow of information is visible, measurable, redirectable, and human-supervisable.

Our long-term vision is two-fold:

**Scientifically,** we aim to build a mathematical foundation for understanding intelligence and, eventually, consciousness.

**Commercially,** we aim to build artificial brain systems that can reason, adapt, make analogies, and remain fully observable and controllable by humans.
