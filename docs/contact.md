## Collaboration

GeometricAI welcomes collaborations with research groups and industry partners working on explainable AI, anomaly detection, healthcare AI, edge intelligence, or related domains.

If your work intersects with the Pursuit of Subspaces framework, or you see a potential application of modular neural architectures in your domain, we would be glad to discuss joint research, technology transfer, or co-development arrangements.

## Prospective Students

If you are interested in a PhD, MSc thesis, or research internship, send the following to [mehmet.yamac@tuni.fi](mailto:mehmet.yamac@tuni.fi) with the subject line `[Prospective Student] Your Name`:

1. A CV including relevant coursework and any prior research experience
2. A short statement (1-2 paragraphs) on your research interests and how they relate to GeometricAI
3. Links to any relevant code, papers, or technical writing
