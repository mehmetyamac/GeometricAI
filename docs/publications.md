## Pursuit of Subspaces (PoS)

**Pursuit of Subspaces (PoS)** is the scientific foundation of Geometric AI, an axiomatic mathematical framework that models neural networks as structured geometric systems. Drawing from the principles of Euclidean geometry, PoS formulates network behaviour through four geometric postulates, providing a unified explanation for representation, computation, and generalisation across architectures.

Within this framework, each layer or module acts as a geometric operator projecting information into progressively refined subspaces. Reasoning becomes traceable, explainability is structural rather than approximate, and capabilities such as zero-shot generalisation, counterfactual explanation, and privacy-preserving transformation emerge naturally from the formulation.

## Research Areas

The PoS framework underpins research across six interconnected directions:

- **Explainability by design:** neural architectures whose internal structure makes decisions interpretable without approximation
- **Zero-shot and few-shot reasoning:** generalisation to new classes and domains from geometric properties alone
- **Adaptive neural computation:** dynamic routing through submodule chains based on input geometry
- **Geometric counterfactual explanations:** minimum-distance transformations from anomalous to normal representations
- **Privacy-preserving transformation:** orthogonal projection to remove sensitive attributes while preserving task structure
- **Controllable human-AI alignment:** steering model behaviour through explicit geometric constraints rather than RLHF
